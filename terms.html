Terms of service

Activity Calendar is a desktop planning tool for personal use. Its Google Calendar connection is optional and uses the permissions you grant through Google.

Choose the destination calendar and review the activity details before sending. Anyone with access to the destination calendar may be able to view the details you send. Keep backups of your activity database and documents.

Google Calendar is a separate service governed by Google’s terms. You can disconnect the app at any time. For support, use the email shown on the Activity Calendar Google consent screen.
