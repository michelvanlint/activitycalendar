Activity Calendar

Activity Calendar is a Windows desktop app for planning activities, trips, and stays. It keeps schedules, notes, and attachments together.

You can connect a Google account and choose a calendar. When you select Send / update Google Calendar, the app sends the activity details and summary to that calendar. Later sends can update the same event. The app reads your calendar list so you can pick a destination and can look up an existing event when you choose to link it.
