Privacy policy

This policy covers the Activity Calendar desktop app and its optional Google Calendar connection.

Google account and Calendar data

When you connect your account, the app reads your calendar list and calendar access roles to help you select a writable calendar. When you choose to find an existing event or update a linked event, it reads event details and identifiers needed for that operation. When you request an upload, it sends the activity title, dates, times, location where applicable, and formatted summary to Google Calendar. The summary can include your notes, travel and accommodation details, costs, and web links. Local attachment files are not uploaded.

Storage and sharing

The app stores activities and linked calendar and event identifiers in your configured SQLite database. It stores Google OAuth credentials and the refresh token in Windows Credential Manager. The app sends activity information directly to Google Calendar when requested. Event details are visible according to the sharing settings of the calendar you choose. The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Disconnecting and deleting data

Use Google connection… → Forget this connection to remove saved authorization from this computer. You can also revoke access in your Google Account connections. Manage uploaded entries in Google Calendar and local activity records and documents in your configured storage. Disconnecting does not delete entries already in Google Calendar.
